# Style Bible V1 — 3D Plastic Toy Diorama

## Visual language
Educational 3D miniature diorama, molded matte plastic and subtle glossy highlights, softly rounded edges, precision toy assembly, studio softboxes, clean neutral backgrounds, accessible engineering explanatory look, physically plausible motion. Modular brick-like forms **inspired by construction toys, not branded LEGO trademarks or minifigure likenesses**.

## Recurring locks
- **Environment:** miniature industrial facility, simplified pipes/valves/control panels, color-coded parts only if report confirms categories; generic labels rather than real plant tags.
- **People:** adult worker toy figurines with neutral facial features, helmet/PPE as appropriate to the documented scene; consistent color outfit and scale.
- **Camera:** miniature tabletop eye-height or three-quarter isometric view; stable or slow single-axis dolly; no chaotic cuts inside one 6-second clip.
- **Light:** soft even lighting; no photoreal skin, grime, cinematic disasters or intense bloom.
- **Palette:** cool off-white, muted slate blue, warm safety orange accents; adjust to distinguish operations without claiming official plant color standards.
- **Format:** 16:9 widescreen initially; same focal length, scale and orientation between adjacent start/end frames.
- **Semantics:** diagrams may simplify real equipment geometry, but operational sequence must not be invented. Use on-screen caption for reconstructed schematic situations.

## Global visual prompt (EN)
High-quality 3D educational toy diorama, small molded plastic modular industrial equipment, smooth rounded hard-surface geometry, tactile matte ABS-like material, subtle specular highlights, clean studio soft lighting, precise miniature construction, coherent scale, controlled camera, neutral background, easily readable action, friendly serious instructional tone, widescreen 16:9. Generic unbranded toy design, no logos, no realistic skin, no real-world plant identifiers.

## Global negative prompt (EN)
photorealism, live action, realistic human skin, official power plant logos, trademarked minifigure face, gore, explosion, fire unless factually verified, frantic movement, extra limbs, object morphing, pipe topology changes, arbitrary warning lights, illegible text, watermarks, dramatic disaster imagery, inconsistent colors, incorrect equipment connections.

## Frame continuity
Keep all static objects anchored; change **only one clearly defined physical state** from START to END. Do not move camera and object simultaneously unless a shot card deliberately specifies it. Ending frame must be physically reachable from the starting frame.

## CASE-004+ master-first continuity (2026-10-20)
Before scene-specific frames, approve stable IDs for LOC (location), EQ (equipment scale/topology), CHAR (people, wardrobe, exactly two arms/two hands), CTRL (control displays). Derive each individual START and END from approved masters. Keep static background and camera identical within a shot. Avoid exaggerated LEGO-like figures, fake technical readouts and extra headings; exact text is post-production overlay. Use Wan 3.0 8s 1080p settings per [NEXT_CASE_RUNBOOK.md](NEXT_CASE_RUNBOOK.md).
